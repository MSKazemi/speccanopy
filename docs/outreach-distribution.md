## Outreach channels in recommended order

1. **Existing professional contacts / voluntary network sharing.** The maintainer should share Issue #3 with architects or staff/principal engineers who may independently review the e-commerce pilot. Ask for a public, non-sensitive expression of interest; follow with private screening and consent. A small number of personally relevant invitations is preferable to unsolicited bulk messages.
2. **Professional social channels where the maintainer has an account**, such as a personal LinkedIn post. Explicitly identify project affiliation and that this is a **voluntary research pilot using synthetic data**, with no compensation arranged. Link to Issue #3 and invite substantive criticism, not endorsements. Publication requires the maintainer's own authorized account and applicable community rules; **not posted from ChatGPT or GitHub at this point**.
3. **Hacker News**, only if the maintainer has a genuine technical question about independent architecture evaluation and writes a human-authored contribution in their own voice. [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) discourage promotional use, forbid soliciting upvotes/comments, and instruct against AI-generated/AI-edited submissions. The [FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html) describes text-based Ask HN submissions. Do **not** copy an AI-written solicitation into HN or use HN primarily for recruitment marketing.
4. **Specialized communities**, only after verifying each current moderation policy and that volunteer research requests are welcome. In particular, do **not** use r/softwarearchitecture for an uninvited promotional or volunteer-recruitment post: reported community rules forbid promotion of personal/business services and job posts (verify the current community rules if circumstances change). Do not cold-post recruitment comments to unrelated repositories or issues.

No third-party community postings, direct invitations, or emails are claimed.

## Follow-up workflow

1. Watch for substantive new comments in Issues #3 and #4. A user's private ChatGPT condition-watch automation may alert the project coordinator; it does not enroll or contact volunteers, and it must not be mistaken for study consent.
2. For each interested person, reply publicly only with a thank-you and a request to use an appropriate **private** channel after the person has opted in. Do not request public contact details, private application briefs, identity documents or signed consent in GitHub issues.
3. Screen experience and conflicts privately; establish actual retention, privacy and publication terms **before** taking personal data or sending confidential cases.
4. Run the [Phase 7 packet preparation](phase7-expert-panel.md) only for people who explicitly agree. The synthetic e-commerce study is a logistics pilot, not a secret independent benchmark.
5. Preserve raw review disagreement; independently validate reviewer qualifications/independence outside Speccanopy. For genuine validation aim for at least two distinct verified independent reviewers per candidate.
6. Do not close Issue #2 or change evidence status until its underlying human-review and permission milestones are actually met.

## When to revisit

At the first substantive response, assess its scope and privacy needs. If no one responds after one or two weeks, adjust channel choice and clarity; do not automatically mass-message, repost repeatedly, or fabricate reviewer participation. Record outreach channels and dates with the coordinator, but **never** commit personal contact data or confidential reviewer records.
